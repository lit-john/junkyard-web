Controls

Desktop browser with a mouse and keyboard (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). Click the game to capture the mouse and look around; WASD to move, Shift to run, Space to jump. Esc releases the mouse; click the game again to recapture it. The first load is about 25 MB.
